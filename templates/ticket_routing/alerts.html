Alert policy

Decides what a monitoring alert does: open a ticket, join one, wait, or only be recorded. Legacy (the default) keeps today's behaviour: one ticket per Rem0te alert, none for synced RMM alerts. The policy never starts a remote session and never asks for an endpoint action. Not AI.

{% if settings.mode == 'legacy' %}
The alert policy is off. Alerts follow the legacy behaviour until you switch it on. To set it up:
  1. set Mode to Alert policy and pick what happens when no rule matches;
  2. tick Also synced RMM alerts if alerts from your RMM sync should open or join tickets too;
  3. add rules for the alerts that need something other than the default, then Save. Rules can be written while the policy is off; they take effect once it is on.
