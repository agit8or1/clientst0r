Superuser Approval

A superuser controls the whole installation. With this policy on, one administrator requests a superuser grant — by creating a superuser or promoting an account on the user page or in Django admin — and a different administrator approves it. The account is not a superuser until then. Turning the policy off is also a protected change. Grants made on the host (manage.py createsuperuser, the Django shell) are outside the application; they are recorded and announced, not blocked. Background: docs/CLIENT_DATA_PROTECTION.md.
