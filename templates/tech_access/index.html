Technician Access

What each MSP technician may do with ticket AI and RMM tools, per customer. A technician has nothing here until assigned a preset at a customer. Staff, admin and superuser flags never grant a preset. Customer portal users are outside technician access. Ordinary ticket work — viewing, manual replies, internal notes — keeps its existing permissions.

Order of evaluation: locked safeguards → containment → explicit denies → customer restrictions → integration availability → the best of the technician's live assignments at that customer, each evaluated with its own scope.

Narrowing applies at once. Anything that widens access — a preset above L1, a group member added, a deny or restriction lifted, looser detection — becomes a protected change: you confirm it with your password, and a different administrator approves it. {% if not can_widen %}There is no second approver yet, so nothing can be widened. Add another superuser first.{% endif %}
