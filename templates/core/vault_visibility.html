Vault Visibility

Since 3.17.653 a technician sees a customer's vault entries — titles, usernames, counts, search results, API and browser-extension results — only where a membership grants vault_view, or where their global role grants it for every customer. Before, MSP staff with no global role saw every customer's vault. This read-only list shows who lost that cross-customer visibility. Nothing here grants access.

To restore access on purpose, add the technician as a member of the customer with a role that includes vault_view, or give them a global role that includes it (Access Management). Seeing an entry and revealing its password stay separate: revealing also needs vault_view_password.
