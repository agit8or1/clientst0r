Documents (SharePoint/OneDrive)

Lets people browse, link and open files in this tenant's OneDrive and SharePoint, and create, import or export copies when they ask to. Each person signs in to Microsoft separately, so Microsoft's own permissions apply to everything they do. The tenant-read and mail features of this connection keep their current permissions ({{ tenant_read_scope }}, client credentials); this section never changes them.
