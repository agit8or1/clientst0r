Weekly security alert digest

Once a week, each opted-in contact of a customer gets a list of that customer's security alerts that are still open: severity, title, device and when it was first seen. Nothing else from the alert is included. Customers with nothing open get no e-mail. Contacts can opt in or out themselves from portal preferences, and every digest has an unsubscribe link.
