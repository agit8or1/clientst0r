LDAP group mappings

Map a directory group to a customer and a role. At every LDAP sign-in, members of a mapped group get an active membership in that customer with that role; when they leave every mapped group for the customer, the membership the mapping created is deactivated. Memberships created by hand are never changed by a mapping. Every change is audit-logged. Group names are matched without regard to case; nested groups count when the group type is Active Directory.
