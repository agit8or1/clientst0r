An access group gives each of its MSP staff members one role at a set of customers, on top of their own access (global role and memberships). Removing someone from a group, or deleting the group, takes that access away at once. Groups hold staff only, never set superuser or staff flags and never grant an RMM tier. A group can also receive technician access presets on the Technician Access page.
