Roles

Roles grant permissions; they never take any away. A member's permissions are the sum of the roles they hold in this organization; a role in one organization gives nothing in another. Which records a role applies to is the membership's record scope (and, for MSP staff, their client scope). Built-in roles are starting points: clone one to customize it.
